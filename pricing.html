Common Pricing Questions

No setup fees for Starter and Professional plans. Enterprise plans may include a one-time onboarding fee based on the complexity of the migration, which will be quoted during your custom assessment.

You can easily scale up at any time. Additional boxes beyond your plan limit are billed at the same per-box rate. We'll notify you when you're approaching your limit so you can upgrade if needed.

Starter plans are month-to-month with no minimum commitment. Professional plans have a 12-month minimum. Enterprise agreements are customized based on your needs, typically 24 or 36 months.

Free retrievals include standard next-business-day delivery of individual boxes or files to your office. Same-day and emergency retrievals are available as add-ons at the rates shown above.
